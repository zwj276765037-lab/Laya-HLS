2026-10-13: CT0 has a scoped generated-RTL PASS for its 32-entry diagnostic table. It is independent of production owner/CLAIM/publish/retire and does not close M003/M004. The prior FullRuntime executor COST_STOP remains unchanged.

The generated RTL normal trace covers table entries 0 and 31, all 32 occupied/read back, physical WI/DEC_FF alias, wrong epoch/spec/identity, TYPE_ROWS valid bit, coverage scope, readers 255/0, same-address and different-address order, fixture lock after first operation, reject-without-write and response hold. The two negative processes set request/model validity off separately and deliberately mismatch the stored owner identity from the live context; original RELEASE_READ still succeeds under its narrower old-reader rule. All 5009 semantic record bits, including both 2048-bit maps, are compared on readback. The 73,429 diagnostic cycles are **not** production control latency; they include 131 serialized diagnostic round trips and controlled backpressure.

AT004 host passed but HLS did not start because the guard rejected its artifact-root/cwd relation. AT005 HLS failed at Vitis aggregate FIFO ports exceeding 4096 bits; a single semantic-preserving repair split the diagnostic port into fixed 512-bit beats, after which AT006 generated. AT007 and AT010 stopped before RTL tool launch on runner checks. AT008 and AT009 passed normal finite RTL, but AT008's initial `held_cycles` counted low `full_n` even with no pending result; AT009 corrected that counter. AT011 first added inactive-context traces; AT012 added stale stored identity to those traces without changing the frozen generated DUT. Original receipts are preserved.
