# Read-only layer-0 Encoder binding for the next design gate

This is architecture/data-binding evidence only. It does not implement Encoder integration and does not claim a real producer chain has executed. The current type-add slice begins after the 28-layer Encoder and LN_FINAL, at an external native HIDDEN checkpoint. The layer-0 chain below is a separate future decision.

The authoritative source locations checked on 2026-10-09 are [04 §4, §7 and §7.1](../../../../vivado/design_source/04_model_package_and_memory_map.md), [10 §1–4](../../../../vivado/design_source/10_rope_and_mask.md), [11 §1–3](../../../../vivado/design_source/11_attention_and_fp_engine.md), and [15 §11, §12, §15](../../../../vivado/design_source/15_integration_interfaces_and_pipeline.md). Actual call and owner behavior is in [Controller lines 205–383, 585–615](../../../../hls/control/full_forward_controller.cpp), [QKV address mapping](../../../../hls/common/laya_memory_map.cpp), and [attention task checks](../../../../hls/operators/full_tasks_attention.cpp). Existing data provenance is the [D2 fixture report](../../d2_controller_reachable/tb/fixture_v2/fixture_report.json).

| --- | --- | --- |
| External `embedding_norm` HIDDEN | Controller checkpoints the post-`LN_EMB` HIDDEN before its 28-layer loop; layer0 uses this HIDDEN directly as `att_source`, while later layers use `LN_ATTN` NORM. TOKEN B1/S64 HIDDEN rows use STATE0/STATE1 base0 and physical stride1024 rows. | A native capture-derived source must be bound to the actual `embedding_norm` node; the current `type_src0` is post-Encoder and cannot be relabeled. |
| `LIN_QKV` | `linear(Op::LIN_QKV,{1,0},att_source,stage2,layer0,...)` loads the compiled family1/layer0 Q8 weight, then for each 16-row tile issues `QUANT` and `LIN_QKV`; it claims and publishes both QKV shards only after full raw coverage. | The existing `norm.f32.bin` and `qkv_gold.f32.bin` in the D2 fixture are derived from native `ir_1414` and independent head QKV0 weight/bias, but their role as layer-0 Encoder ingress is **not established**. They are useful numeric provenance, not this chain's source/gold binding. |
| Raw QKV pair | QKV bank0/bank1 mean even/odd head shard, not interchangeable whole matrices. Address is `(((((plane*2+batch)*8+head/2)*1024+token)*64)+d)*4`, with shard `head%2`; each BO reserves 12 MiB for F32 `[3,2,8,1024,64]`. The pair has matching stage2/layer0/request/epoch, `Content::QKV_RAW`, and whole-layer coverage before publish. | Future TB must test both shard refs, Q/K/V planes, head0/15 and row0/63, and delay final raw write retirement before permitting RoPE. |
| `LOAD_ROPE` and pair transition | For layer0, `mode=layer%3==0` selects full table bank0. `LOAD_ROPE` loads `2*1025*64*4` bytes; `full_begin_qkv_rope_pair` leases both raw shards as a pair. For all 16 heads, B and 16-row tiles, ROPE reads full 64-feature Q and K heads and writes rotated Q/K in place; V stays raw. `full_commit_qkv_rope_pair` publishes both refs with epoch+1 and `Content::QKV_FINAL`. | No prefilling QKV or forcing FINAL is allowed. Raw-to-final is an actual whole-pair exclusive transition; final-read checks must reject half-complete Q/K or an unmatched shard. |
| `LOAD_KV` → QK/softmax/AV | For each head, Controller issues `LOAD_KV` (K and V planes, `2*B*S*64*4` bytes), then for each 16-query tile QK, inplace SCORE→PROB softmax, and AV into ATT_OUT. QK task checks both final QKV refs, requested head shard, fully ready KV, mask context, and complete score owner. Attention output publishes only after all heads/tiles. | Existing cold Attention v5 proves its own preloaded four-command window, not this `LIN_QKV`/RoPE production connection. Its metadata/reset results and the Q8 R0 adapter status must be carried into the next gate. |

A finite future source contract can stage this without a full request: first bind an authentic `embedding_norm` HIDDEN and family1/layer0 Q8 model ports, prove QUANT/LIN_QKV produces the raw pair; next prove real LOAD_ROPE, all-head pair commit and FINAL epoch; then connect LOAD_KV/QK/softmax/AV for a bounded B1/S64 head/query window. The second and third stages depend on the former's actual bytes and ownership. B1/S64 still requires 16 heads and 64 rows for the pair transition as currently coded; a single head diagnostic cannot claim the full `attention` method. Any execution proposal needs exact source closure, independent gold provenance, actual physical schedule, finite cycle/page/RSS budget, and a new Astra gate. Q8 R0 primitive adapter and Attention metadata/reset evidence remain unresolved dependencies, not facts supplied by this appendix.
